NEON RUN Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

NEON RUN is an offline arcade game. The app does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, preferences, scores, and in-game currency are stored locally on your device using browser storage. This information is not sent to the developer.

Third-party services

The game does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, third-party account services, or network features.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the GitHub repository: github.com/Abolfazlsoltan/Neon.
